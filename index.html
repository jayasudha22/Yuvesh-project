🧵 Fibers and Their Sources 🌿

Cotton

Cotton is a natural fiber from cotton plants. It is soft and used for clothes.

Silk

Silk comes from silkworms. It is smooth, shiny, and used for sarees.

Jute

Jute comes from jute plants and is used to make ropes and bags.

Wool

Wool comes from sheep and keeps us warm.

Nylon

Nylon is a man-made fiber produced in factories.
