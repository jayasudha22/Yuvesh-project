🧵 Fibers and Their Sources 🌿

Cotton

Cotton comes from cotton plants. It is soft and used to make clothes.

Silk

Silk comes from silkworms. It is smooth and shiny.

Jute

Jute comes from jute plants. It is used to make ropes and bags.

Wool

Wool comes from sheep. It keeps us warm.

Nylon

Nylon is a man-made fiber made in factories.
